About Lizmart Clinic

Founded with a vision to bring accessible, high-quality healthcare to the Effurun community, Lizmart Clinic has grown into a trusted name in family medicine. Our team of dedicated professionals is committed to providing personalized care in a warm, welcoming environment.

From routine checkups to specialized treatments, we combine medical expertise with genuine compassion — because we believe every patient deserves to feel heard and valued.
